## Description
```
LastPouch helps you stop using nicotine pouches — your way.

GO COLD TURKEY, OR TAPER DOWN
Some people quit in one day. Others need stairs, not a cliff. Build a personal taper plan from your current habit: the app creates a week-by-week ladder that first lowers how many pouches you use each day, then lowers the strength, until your Zero Day arrives. Prefer to stop today? Track a clean streak toward 90 days nicotine-free instead.

A CEILING, NOT A TARGET
Your daily limit is a maximum, not a quota. Log each pouch with one tap. Going over doesn't erase your progress — the plan can hold a step and keep going.

WHEN A CRAVING HITS
The Panic Button talks you through the next five minutes — because that's how long a craving actually lasts. Cold water, a walk, a breathing pattern, a plan.

SEE IT WORKING
• Streak, slips, and days to nicotine-free at a glance
• Your taper ladder, week by week, with nicotine per day trending down
• Money saved, calculated from your real usage
• A daily journal of every log

LEARN WHAT'S HAPPENING
Short, plain-language lessons: how nicotine hooks your brain, what to expect day by day after you stop, why sleep gets rough (then better), what pouches do to your gums, and how to replace the ritual — not just the drug.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads. Your data is never transmitted anywhere.

LastPouch provides general information and self-tracking tools. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. If you have health concerns about nicotine use or quitting, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.

Not affiliated with any nicotine pouch brand.
```
