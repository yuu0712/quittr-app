Support — Quit Pouches

Need help with Quit Pouches? Email us at info@logica-solutions.jp. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Your data

All of your data (streak, taper plan, journal) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes the data. See our Privacy Policy.

Resetting the app

Use the Reset button on the Home tab to restart your streak, or end your plan from Profile → My Plan.

Health note

Quit Pouches is a self-help tracking tool, not a medical service. If quitting nicotine is affecting your health, please talk to a doctor or pharmacist.
