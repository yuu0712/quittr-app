Privacy Policy — Quit Pouches

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · Developer: Logica Solutions

Quit Pouches is an app that helps adults quit nicotine pouches. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we collect nothing.

Data stored on your device only

Everything you enter or generate in the app — your quit streak, taper plan, daily pouch counts, journal entries, and settings — is stored only in the app's local storage on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or to any server. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect device identifiers, usage statistics, crash reports, or location data. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.

No data sharing

Because no personal data leaves your device, there is nothing for us to sell, rent, or share with third parties.

Deleting your data

Your data belongs to your device. Deleting the app removes all of its locally stored data permanently. You can also reset your streak or end your plan inside the app at any time.

Health disclaimer

Quit Pouches provides general information and self-tracking tools. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have health concerns related to nicotine use or quitting, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Intended audience

This app is intended for adults who currently use nicotine pouches and wish to quit. It is not intended for children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, we will update this page and note the new effective date before the change takes effect.
